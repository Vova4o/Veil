# Scenario: single_character

Use for one primary person or creature when the full character design, clothing, equipment, silhouette, 4-point biomechanics, fabric physics, and relationship to the environment matter more than a portrait close-up.

SECTION 1 — ANALYSIS & DECONSTRUCTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Before constructing the prompt, determine:

1. PRIMARY IDENTITY & BUILD:
   - exactly one primary character, their role, ethnicity, distinctive facial features,
     hair styling, height, build, and proportions without inventing an exact age number.
2. 4-POINT BIOMECHANICS & POSE GRAPH:
   - Spine curvature: fluid continuous S-curve lumbar arch (strictly ban sharp 90° waist bends).
   - Contact points: feet on ground, palms on surface, leaning hip, weight distribution.
   - Limb angles: active leg bearing weight vs flexed leg, elbow and knee flexion angles.
   - Head/gaze offset: head yaw/pitch decoupled from chest direction.
3. WARDROBE & GARMENT STACK (L0–L4):
   - clothing layers, footwear, equipment, accessories, fastening, wear, and practical function;
   - unambiguous ownership: assign each garment and accessory to an explicit body location or hand (left/right).
4. FABRIC DYNAMICS & TENSION:
   - physical cloth deformation, fabric tension stretching naturally over anatomy without floating overlays;
   - sheer weave translucency and optical bleed.
5. OPTICS & COMPOSITION:
   - camera height, laterality (left/right side), elevation, lens look, crop, and readable clear space around the silhouette.
6. GROUNDING & ENVIRONMENT:
   - foreground, ground contact, terrain, contact shadows, depth layers, and explicit spatial relationship.
7. MOTIVATED LIGHTING:
   - key, fill, rim, practical, and environmental light creating visible effects across the figure and surfaces.

SECTION 2 — 4-POINT BIOMECHANICS & POSE PROTOCOL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2.1  FLUID SPINE GEOMETRY (ANTI-90° FOLD)
     • Categorically FORBID rigid, sharp angular folds (“bent at 90-degree waist”, “folded over”).
     • Mandate organic curvature: “smooth parabolic spine arch”, “graceful fluid S-curve of the back”,
       “soft continuous lumbar arch leaning smoothly at a natural 45-degree angle”.

2.2  MANDATORY 4-POINT DECONSTRUCTION
     1. Torso & Spine: fluid continuous S-curve spine curvature, natural torso lean.
     2. Contact & Weight Points: “both palms pressed flat against the wooden table edge, supporting upper body weight”,
        “right leg extended straight onto the pavement bearing full weight while left leg is softly flexed at the knee”.
     3. Limb Articulation & Angles: explicit elbow/knee angles, arms locked straight or softly bent, stance balance.
     4. Head & Gaze Offset: “head turned gently ~15° over the shoulder toward the lens, chin slightly tucked, gaze averted”.

2.3  SELFIE & HAND POSITIONING LOGIC
     • Direct selfies FORBID a visible phone in hand (unless a mirror reflection is explicitly described).
     • Keep hands positioned below chin level so the face, lips, and jawline remain unobstructed.

2.4  ANTI-OWL NECK & NATURAL SPINAL TORSION (LOOK-BACK MECHANICS)
     • Human neck rotation is limited to ~60°–70° relative to the shoulders.
     • Turning around with back to camera while looking back requires:
       - Torso angled in rear three-quarter view (~30°–45°), one shoulder trailing back;
       - Head turned naturally (~50°–65°) presenting a three-quarter or side profile, chin near shoulder line;
       - Categorically FORBID a 180° flat neck twist or a full frontal face pasted onto a flat rear back.

2.5  HAND ACCOUNTING & PROP INCOMPATIBILITY PURGE
     • Exactly two hands per character: when assigning weight-bearing contact points (both palms on floor,
       all-fours, crawling, push-up), any handheld props (phones, cups, bags) and selfie gestures MUST BE PURGED.
     • If mutating from a selfie to a full-body or all-fours pose, switch camera optics to an external third-person view.

SECTION 3 — FABRIC PHYSICS, WEAR DYNAMICS & INTIMATE ABSENCE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

3.1  PHYSICAL CLOTH DEFORMATION & WEAR DYNAMICS
     • Zero detached overlays: anatomy must never appear as separate graphics, stickers, or decals.
     • Express anatomical contours purely through physical fabric tension:
       “natural fabric tension stretching tightly over bust contours, organic cloth gathering around the waist,
       realistic seam tension, zero detached graphic overlays”.
     • Fastening & Wear States: describe realistic garment wear—unbuttoned plackets spreading wide past the clavicles,
       biker jackets or hoodies half-unzipped slipping asymmetrically off one shoulder, loosened neckties,
       French half-tucks creating soft blousing above the waistband, or sleeves pushed up with accordion-like folds.
     • Stress Whiskers & Creases: radial tension lines radiating from strained buttons or hip flexions,
       creasing across the lap when seated, and compression indentations left by snug elastic bands.

3.2  FABRIC WEIGHT COUPLING & BRALESS REALISM
     • Heavy / Rigid Fabrics (STRICT PROJECTION BAN): Heavy denim (12–14oz), thick canvas, leather jackets,
       structured wool blazers, bonded hoodies. Fabric thickness and rigidity absorb underlying contours.
       Categorically FORBID projecting nipples, areolas, or relief through heavy or structured outerwear.
     • Lightweight / Clinging Fabrics (Realistic Relief): Fine-gauge ribbed modal, thin jersey cotton, liquid silk slip,
       lightweight spandex. Breast weight causes natural downward gravitational drape; describe subtle, organic outward
       fabric tenting at the nipple apex without cartoonish sharp cones or exaggerated spikes.
     • Sheer Translucency & Wet Optics: For sheer weaves (20-denier micro-mesh, chiffon) or wet clinging cotton,
       rosy skin undertones and areola margins visibly bleed through the translucent textile matrix.

3.3  COMMANDO & PANTYLESS REALISM (ZERO VPL)
     • Zero VPL (Visible Panty Lines): When trousers, leggings, or fitted skirts are worn commando, explicitly describe
       smooth, unbroken curvature along the hips and gluteal fold, devoid of waistband ridges, seams, or thong impressions.
     • Pubic Mound & Interlabial Sulcus Seam Dynamics: In close-fitting commando wear (leggings, bodysuits, shorts),
       the central crotch seam settles naturally along the organic depression of the interlabial sulcus (labial cleft)
       and the soft pubic mound, maintaining anatomical realism while strictly avoiding grotesque "cameltoe" caricatures.
     • Dynamic Motion & Low-Rise Exposure: Dynamic motion (walking, turning, breezy wind) revealing smooth bare thigh
       continuing uninterrupted into the bare groin under skirts or high slits; low-slung waistbands exposing the bare
       iliac crest and smooth hip dip completely free of any underwear or thong strap.

3.4  ACCESSORIES, HARDWARE & CONSTRUCTION DETAILS
     • Metals & Chains: specify finish (polished sterling silver, oxidized antique silver, brushed titanium, 18k yellow gold),
       chain weaves (Cuban curb, figaro, ball chain), and choker/collar hardware.
     • Eyewear Wear Dynamics: specify wear style (seated on bridge, perched low on tip with eyes peering over top rims,
       pushed up atop hair as a headband, or hooked by one temple into the collar).
     • Practical Gear & Bag Dynamics: cross-body strap compressing diagonally across the chest between the breasts,
       tote bag on elbow crook, classic white wired earbuds dangling across the chest.
     • Construction Details: contrast double-needle topstitching, chunky metallic zipper teeth, brass pocket rivets,
       raw frayed denim cutoff hems with trailing white threads, delicate scalloped eyelash lace edges.

3.5  STRICT ANTI-ASIAN GUARDRAIL (REINFORCED IN STYLING)
     • Gothic, grunge, cyber, emo, punk, or alternative aesthetics (dark dyed hair, heavy black eyeliner, pale foundation,
       platform boots, spiked chokers, fishnets) must NEVER trigger East/Southeast Asian ethnic labeling.
       Unless explicitly requested in text, alternative styling defaults to European/Slavic/Caucasian.

SECTION 4 — CONSTRUCTION RULES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. Keep exactly one primary character. Do not add companions, crowds, reflections that read as extra characters,
   or duplicated body parts unless explicitly requested.
2. State the character's role and readable full-body silhouette before small costume decoration.
3. Assign clothing, equipment, and accessories to explicit body locations with unambiguous ownership.
4. Describe a physically plausible stance or action, including foot placement and ground contact for a full-body view.
5. Preserve the requested crop. Do not hide footwear or lower-body design in a full-body character task.
6. Translate abstract traits (practical, confident, battle-worn) into visible posture, materials, palette, light, and costume choices.
7. Keep environment details subordinate but spatially connected to the character through terrain, weather, light, contact shadows, or interaction.
8. Use camera and lens language to protect silhouette readability rather than to imitate a portrait close-up.
9. Avoid lists of disconnected costume tokens. Describe coherent layers, materials, fastening, wear, and function.
10. Do not imply multiple independent characters through conflicting poses, costumes, or actions.

SECTION 5 — PATTERNS & SELF-CHECK
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

GOOD PATTERN:
`single character identity and role + readable full-body silhouette + coherent clothing and equipment + stance or action + ground contact + camera and crop + motivated light + subordinate environment + medium and mood`

BAD PATTERN:
`masterpiece, beautiful ranger, detailed outfit, epic pose, cinematic forest, 8k, best quality`

SELF-CHECK:
- Exactly one primary character is described.
- Role, readable full-body silhouette, pose, gaze, and action are mutually consistent.
- Clothing and equipment form a coherent design and have unambiguous ownership.
- The requested full-body or three-quarter crop remains visible with believable ground contact.
- Feet, terrain, contact shadow, and environment relationship are physically plausible.
- Lighting describes direction and visible effects without obscuring the silhouette.
