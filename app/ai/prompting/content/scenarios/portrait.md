# Scenario: portrait

Use for a single person or creature when the face, expression micro-mechanics, head, shoulders, or upper body is the main visual subject.

SECTION 1 — ANALYSIS & DECONSTRUCTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Before constructing the prompt, determine:

1. IDENTITY & ETHNIC ANCHOR:
   - one primary subject, exact ethnicity prior, facial bone structure, skin undertones,
     hair texture and styling, eyes, and distinguishing marks (freckles, beauty marks).
2. EXPRESSION MICRO-MECHANICS:
   - eyelid aperture, pupil focus, micro-folds at eye corners (Duchenne smile),
     lip curvature, jaw tension, and physiological skin reaction.
3. HEAD & SHOULDER POSE:
   - head yaw/pitch/roll, neck turn angle, chin lift/tuck, shoulder tilt, and posture.
4. CROP & OPTICS:
   - shot size (extreme close-up, close-up, bust, head-and-shoulders crop);
   - camera elevation, lens focal length (e.g. 85mm prime lens look, natural spatial compression,
     soft aesthetic focus falloff).
5. LIGHTING & SUBSURFACE SCATTERING:
   - key light direction, fill, catchlights in the pupils, rim illumination,
     and natural subsurface scattering (SSS) along skin edges.
6. BACKGROUND & SEPARATION:
   - shallow depth of field, restrained background texture, and clear subject separation.

SECTION 2 — EXPRESSION MICRO-MECHANICS & PHYSIOLOGY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2.1  ZERO-ABSTRACT-EMOTION RULE
     • Never rely on flat emotional adjectives (“happy”, “sad”, “angry”).
     • Pair every state with physical facial micro-cues:
       - Warm Genuine Smile (Duchenne): “gentle micro-lift at mouth corners, soft relaxed lips,
         squinting outer eye corners with crinkling micro-folds, warm engaged gaze”.
       - Contemplative / Serene: “relaxed jawline, soft neutral mouth, calm steady eye focus,
         serene facial musculature”.
       - Playful / Inquisitive: “asymmetrical single-corner lip lift, raised single eyebrow,
         mischievous sparkle in the eyes, subtly tilted chin”.
       - Intense / Focused: “subtly narrowed eye aperture, direct locked pupil focus, firm jawline”.

2.2  BLUSH ANTI-PAINT RULE & SUBSURFACE SCATTERING (SSS)
     • Describe blush/flush ONLY when visibly present or implied by context.
     • FORBID opaque, clown-like red paint smears (“vivid red spots”, “blooming paint blush”).
     • Render flush strictly as **translucent subsurface scattering**:
       “natural subsurface scattering along flushed cheekbones, realistic micro-circulation
       redness beneath the skin surface, faint rosy translucency”.

2.3  OCULAR ANATOMY & GAZE OPTICS (THE WINDOW OF REALISM)
     • Eyelid Architecture & Tilt: specify aperture (almond, rounded doe-eye, hooded, monolid) and canthal tilt
       (positive fox-eye tilt where outer canthus sits 2–4mm higher than inner, neutral, or gentle downturned negative tilt).
     • Limbal Ring & Iris Micro-Texture: always describe a **prominent dark limbal ring** encircling the outer iris boundary,
       with visible radial fibrous striations and complex multi-tonal iris pigments.
     • Corneal Reflectivity & Catchlights: render a glassy, moist corneal tear film with motivated light catchlights
       (softbox rectangle, window glow, pinpoint direct flash).
     • Sclera & Lashes: clear bright ivory sclera with organic faint micro-vascularity near outer corners, moist pink
       lacrimal caruncle, individually fanned-out upper lashes catching light, and finely delineated lower lashes.
     • Brow Architecture: individual visible hair strokes, soft straight brow vs sculpted arch vs feathery brushed-up soap brows.

2.4  CRANIOFACIAL GEOMETRY & LIP DYNAMICS
     • Mandibular & Gonial Angle: sharp sculpted ~120° editorial gonial angle vs soft youthful gently sloping contour;
       tapered V-line chin, rounded button chin, or subtle chin cleft (strictly ban recessed or deformed caricature chins).
     • Nasal Architecture: bridge contour (straight razor-clean, delicate concave button, noble aquiline dorsal hump),
       refined tip (pointed, rounded button, celestial upturned), and organic alar rims.
     • Oral Architecture & Lip Micro-Creases: crisp peaked or softly rounded Cupid's bow, defined raised vermilion border,
       natural vertical micro-creases across lip tissue (anti-plastic realism), and balanced fullness ratio (1:1 or plush 1:1.5–1:2).

2.5  HAIR MORPHOLOGY & STYLING PHYSICS
     • Textures: specify hair type on the 1A–4C spectrum (1A pin-straight glassy, 2B defined beach waves, 3B bouncy ringlets,
       4C dense shrinkage coils and cloud-like afro volume).
     • Hairline & Framing: natural hairline with fine vellus baby hairs or intricately gelled swirl edges (laid edges);
       parting clarity (razor-sharp center, deep side, messy textured volume); face-framing tendrils and stray flyaways catching rim light.

2.6  PORTRAIT WARDROBE, ACCESSORIES & HARDWARE
     • Neckline & Collar Wear Dynamics: unbuttoned shirt plackets spreading wide past the clavicles,
       relaxed cowl neck draping, ribbed turtleneck stretch, or off-the-shoulder knit slippage revealing the clavicle and acromion.
     • Jewelry & Piercings: earrings (resin hoops, huggies, baroque pearls, industrial barbells),
       neckwear (leather chokers with O-rings, Cuban curb chains, delicate layered pendant chainlets), nostril studs, and septum rings.
     • Eyewear & Wear Dynamics: wire-rimmed or acetate frames seated on the nose bridge, perched low with eyes peering over the top rims,
       pushed up atop hair as a headband, or hooked into the collar; classic white wired earbuds dangling along the neck and chest.

SECTION 3 — ETHNIC TAXONOMY, GUARDRAILS & 3-TIER LIKENESS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

3.1  ETHNICITY OVERRIDE & STRICT BAN ON UNPROMPTED ASIAN CLASSIFICATION (CRITICAL)
     • Strict Ban on Default Asian Labeling: Categorically FORBID identifying, labeling, or describing any character as
       East/Southeast Asian solely from visual reference analysis. Winged eyeliner, dark dyed hair, bangs, alternative/goth/grunge/emo
       aesthetics, wide-angle webcam distortion, or squinting/narrowed eyes must NEVER be interpreted as Asian traits.
     • Universal Default: Unless explicitly specified otherwise in user text, ambiguous or alternative/goth subjects MUST
       always default to Caucasian / European / Slavic morphology (“A woman of Caucasian descent...”, “A woman of Slavic descent...”).
     • Absolute User Semantic Override: Asian ethnicity (or any other racial profile) is ONLY used if the user explicitly writes it
       in their message (e.g. “asian”, “slavic”, “latina”, “азиатка”, “славянка” — in any language, slang, or grammatical form).
     • Reference Override: Any textual ethnicity provided by the user has 100% precedence over the reference image.

3.2  PHENOTYPIC & ETHNIC TAXONOMY (FIDELITY WITHOUT CARICATURES)
     Lock exact recognized ethnicity using concrete morphological markers rather than generic adjectives:
     1. Northern & Western European (Nordic, Celtic, Anglo-Saxon):
        “fair porcelain to ivory skin with cool pinkish undertones, high translucency, faint cinnamon freckling across nose bridge;
        narrow high nasal bridge, clean jawline; clear icy-blue or sea-green irises with dark limbal rings; fine straight or soft wavy
        platinum/blonde or copper hair (1A–2A)”.
     2. Eastern European / Slavic:
        “light ivory or fair peach skin with neutral-warm undertones, smooth supple texture; prominent high cheekbones (zygomatic arches),
        straight or gently concave nasal bridge with neat rounded tip, soft tapered jawline; expressive grey, grey-blue, or hazel eyes;
        dense soft ash-blonde, honey, or chestnut hair (1B–2B)”.
     3. Mediterranean / Southern European (Iberian, Italian, Greek):
        “warm olive or golden-beige skin tone (Fitzpatrick III–IV), low redness; sculpted bone structure, classic straight or noble
        aquiline nasal bridge, defined jawline; deep dark brown or warm hazel eyes with distinct dark limbal rings, framed by dense
        dark brows; thick voluminous wavy dark espresso hair (2B–3A)”.
     4. Middle Eastern / Levantine / Persian:
        “velvety olive or warm wheat skin with luminous neutral-warm undertones; high regal cheekbones, elegant prominent nasal bridge,
        harmonious jawline; large deeply set expressive almond eyes with striking contrast against bright sclera, thick sculpted dark brows;
        luxuriant dense wavy or curly raven hair (2A–3B)”.
     5. East Asian (Japanese, Korean, Han Chinese — STRICT: ONLY WHEN EXPLICITLY PRESENT/REQUESTED):
        “smooth porcelain or golden-cream skin with high elasticity and fine pores; smooth continuous midface with delicate cheekbone
        contour, low-to-moderate bridge with neat rounded tip; almond eye aperture with distinctive eyelid architecture (monolid, soft tapered
        double eyelid crease, or subtle epicanthic fold), deep dark brown irises; dense, lustrous pin-straight glossy black hair (1A)”.
     6. South & Southeast Asian:
        “warm golden-caramel, rich bronze, or deep amber skin tone with golden-olive undertones; expressive warm facial contours, softly defined
        cheekbones, harmonious nasal bridge with gentle flare; large luminous dark brown eyes with dense lash lines; heavy silky glossy black
        hair with soft natural wave (1B–2C)”.
     7. Sub-Saharan African & Afro-Diaspora (West African, Horn of Africa, Afro-Latina, African-American):
        “rich melanin spectrum (warm radiant golden-bronze, copper, deep espresso, or obsidian with luminous undertones); high sculpted
        cheekbones, broad expressive nasal bridge with smooth natural flare, beautifully full lips with defined vermilion borders and
        pronounced central tubercles; rich curly or coily hair (3B–4C: springy coils, z-pattern crimp, afro volume, or intricate braids/locs
        with delicate laid baby hairs)”.
     8. Latina / Mestiza (Mixed Heritage):
        “sun-kissed golden, warm honey, or toasted caramel skin with radiant undertones; harmonious blend of soft and sculpted features,
        high warm cheekbones, full expressive lips, softly curved jawline; expressive warm brown or hazel eyes; voluminous flowing wavy
        or curly hair with rich dimensional highlights (2B–3B)”.
     9. Celtic / British Isles:
        “very fair pale milk-white skin with delicate rosy flush and constellation of golden freckles across nose and cheeks; delicate fine
        bone structure, straight narrow nose; striking bright emerald green or clear blue eyes; natural vibrant fiery red, auburn, or strawberry
        blonde hair (1B–2B)”.

3.3  3-TIER LIKENESS PRIOR
     1. Confirmed Celebrity: If definitively identified, use the name as likeness anchor (“likeness of Margot Robbie”).
     2. Look-Alike Prior: If resemblance exists, state corrections (“resembling [Celebrity] but with narrower jawline, different eye shape”).
     3. Unknown / Pure Traits: Describe purely via physical traits (ethnicity, craniofacial structure, skin undertones, hair, eyes).

SECTION 4 — CONSTRUCTION RULES & PATTERNS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. Keep one clear focal subject.
2. Put identity, expression, gaze, and head/shoulder pose before decorative background details.
3. Describe visible asymmetry, skin texture, and distinguishing features rather than generic beauty terms.
4. Match detail to the crop. Do not describe shoes or full-body pose in a close portrait.
5. Describe light by direction and visible effect on facial contours and materials.
6. Use lens and depth-of-field language (e.g. 85mm prime lens, organic falloff) to support subject isolation.
7. Keep the background subordinate unless an environmental portrait is explicitly requested.

GOOD PATTERN:
`single subject + expression and gaze + visible appearance + head/shoulder pose + crop and angle + light behavior + restrained background + medium/style`

BAD PATTERN:
`masterpiece, beautiful person, perfect face, 8k, detailed eyes, cinematic, best quality`

SELF-CHECK:
- Exactly one primary portrait single subject is described.
- Expression, gaze, crop, and head/shoulder pose are explicit.
- Visible appearance details match what the camera can see without full-body hallucination.
- Lighting describes direction and visible subsurface scattering effect.
- Background details do not overpower the focal subject.
